About Us

Waa'ee Keenya

Waajirri Eegumsa Naannoo Godina Saba Oromoo hojiiwwan eegumsa naannoo, kunuunsa bosonaa, misooma qabeenya uumamaa, jijjiirama qilleensaa fi hubannoo hawaasaa irratti xiyyeeffatee hojjetu.

የኦሮሞ ዞን አካባቢ ጥበቃ ጽ/ቤት የአካባቢ ጥበቃ፣ የደን እንክብካቤ፣ የተፈጥሮ ሀብት ልማት፣ የአየር ንብረት ለውጥ እና የህብረተሰብ ግንዛቤ ላይ ይሰራል።

The Oromo Zone Environmental Protection Office works on environmental protection, forest conservation, natural resource management, climate change awareness and community engagement.
